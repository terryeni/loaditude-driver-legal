Loaditude Driver — Support

Loaditude Driver is the companion app for professional drivers using the Loaditude transport and warehouse platform. This page tells you how to get help.

Getting an account

The app requires an account provided by your employer or transport operator. If you do not have sign-in details, please contact your dispatch or operations team. We cannot create driver accounts directly; they are set up by your operator.

Contact support

For help with the app, email support@wms360.io. Please include your name, your operator's company name, and a short description of the issue. We aim to respond within one business day.

Common questions

I can't sign in

Check that you are using the email and password your operator gave you, and that you have a working internet connection. If it still fails, contact your dispatch team or email support.

Location or camera isn't working

Open iOS Settings, find Loaditude Driver, and make sure Location and Camera permissions are allowed. Background location is needed so dispatch can follow your run while you drive.

I'm not getting job notifications

In iOS Settings, ensure Notifications are enabled for Loaditude Driver. If they are on and you still miss alerts, contact support.
