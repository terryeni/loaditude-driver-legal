Loaditude Driver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

Loaditude Driver ("the app") is a companion app for professional drivers working with the Loaditude transport and warehouse platform, operated by Agile Without Borders LTD ("we", "us"). The app is used under an account provided by your employer or transport operator. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Location data (precise), including in the background. While you are signed in and on duty, the app reports your device location so your dispatch team can follow the progress of your run, including while the app is in the background or your phone is locked. You control this through the on/off duty control in the app and your device location permission.
  • Photos you capture. When you record proof of collection or delivery, the photos you take are uploaded and attached to that job. The app uses the camera and, if you choose, your photo library only for this purpose.
  • Account identifier and job activity. Your driver account id and the actions you take in the app (jobs viewed, time logged, vehicle checks, proof submitted) are sent to the Loaditude backend to run the service.
  • Push notification token. A device token is used to send you job alerts.

The app talks only to the Loaditude backend over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use it

We use this information solely to operate the service for your employer or operator: to show you your jobs and runs, keep dispatch updated on your location during a run, record proof of collection and delivery, track your time and vehicle checks, and send you job notifications.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising or for tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sharing

Your information is available to the operator whose account you use (your employer or the transport company you drive for). We use service providers (for hosting and file storage) to run the platform on our behalf; they process data under contract and only to provide the service.

Retention

Job, location and proof records are retained for as long as your operator's account is active and as needed for their operational and legal requirements, then deleted in line with our platform retention policy.

Your choices

  • You can turn location reporting off by going off duty in the app or revoking the location permission in iOS Settings (this may limit dispatch features).
  • You can manage camera, photos and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • To access, correct or delete your personal data, contact us or your operator using the details below.
